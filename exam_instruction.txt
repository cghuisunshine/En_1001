Final Exam
Your final invigilated exam is 3 hours in length. It will have three parts: grammar and punctuation (multiple choice), a passage analysis, and an essay answer.

Do not attempt the Final Exam until you have completed and submitted all assignments, have had adequate time for review, and have thoroughly practiced. Note that final exams are not returned to students in order to maintain exam integrity.

The final exam will test your knowledge of the following: any of the short stories covered in the course modules, the novel Pride and Prejudice, and your mastery of English language grammar. You should be familiar with the short stories from the course modules, including title, author, their plots, themes and other literary elements. You should also be familiar with the novel.
